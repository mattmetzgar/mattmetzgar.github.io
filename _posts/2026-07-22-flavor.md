I﻿ was able to catch up on some reading and read books such as The End of Overeating and Foods that Lie.  A common theme from the books is that flavor is the dominant attraction for eating junk food.

N﻿ow this is somewhat obvious, but it does lead to some other insights.  These insights are developed in the excellent book [Nourishment](https://www.amazon.com/Nourishment-Animals-Rediscovering-Nutritional-Wisdom/dp/1603588027/) by Fred Provenza.  He discusses how flavor in whole foods is an indicator of its nutritional value.  For example, if an apple tastes good, it is probably in in-season and at its height of nutrient density.

T﻿his flavor-nutrition relationship has been taken advantage of with modern processed foods.  Now you can eat some cookies that taste great but have no nutritional value.
